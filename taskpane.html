Before sending

If your email says "attached" but nothing is, Send asks first. A shared SharePoint or OneDrive link counts as attached.

How it works

Write as usual. When you select Send, the add-in restyles your part of the email in the Versatex look; earlier messages in a thread are left alone. Every word you wrote arrives: if styling would drop one, the email goes with basic styling instead.

Subject tags

  • [review] pauses once so you can check the styled email, then sends with a clean subject.
  • [plain] sends the email exactly as typed.

Tips

  • Select Apply Versatex style in the message toolbar to style the draft now and check it before you send.
  • Your own colours, sizes and code keep their look; only Outlook's default text is restyled.
  • Pasting from Word, Teams or a document is fine: lists keep their shape.
  • Emails written in the Outlook phone app go out unstyled; they look fine when read on a phone.
